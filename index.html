    Types of Panda

    Giant Panda

    Scientific name: Ailuropoda melanoleuca

    • Black & white fur
    • Eats mostly bamboo
    • Lives in mountain forest of China
      • Know More

    Red Panda

    Scientific name: Ailurus fulgens

    • Reddish-brown fur and a bushy tail
    • Eats bamboo, fruits, and other foods
    • Lives in Himalayan and nearby mountain forests

    Panda Habitat

    Bamboo Forests

    Bamboo provides most of a panda’s food and is essential for survival.

    Mountain Homes

    The majority of wild pandas live in the Minshan and Qinling mountain ranges.

    Safe Shelter

    Trees and dense vegetation offer places to rest and shelter.

    Habitat Protection

    Protecting forests helps pandas find food and survive in the wild.

    Panda Fun Facts

    Bamboo lovers

    Giant pandas eat bamboo for most of their diet, although they belong to the order Carnivora.

    Big Appetites

    Pandas can spend around 10–16 hours a day feeding.

    Mountain homes

    Wild giant pandas live in mountain forests in China.

    A special wrist

    Pandas have an enlarged wrist bone that helps them grip bamboo.

    Tiny newborns

    Newborn panda cubs are extremely small compared with their mothers.
